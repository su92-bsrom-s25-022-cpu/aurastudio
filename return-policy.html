7-Day Exchange Privilege

At Aura Studio, your total contentment is our foremost priority. We understand that finding your ideal silhouette and fit is essential. Consequently, we provide a seamless 7-day exchange window for all eligible prêt apparel and accessories delivered across Pakistan.

1. Stitched Lawn Suits & Prêt Exchange

Should your stitched suit require a size adjustment or style exchange, we gladly process size exchanges provided the following criteria are satisfied:

  • The item is in its pristine original condition: unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and free of perfumes or makeup marks.
  • All original designer brand tags, ribbons, and garment bags remain intact.
  • Exchange request is initiated within 7 calendar days of courier delivery.

2. Turkish Jewelry & Pouches

Due to personal hygiene considerations and the delicate artisan craftsmanship of 24k micro-gold plating, earrings and piercings are non-returnable unless received in a damaged or defective state upon parcel opening. Necklaces, bracelets, rings, and handcrafted pouches may be exchanged within 7 days in their original velvet presentation box.

3. Defective or Incorrect Deliveries

In the rare event that an item arrives with a textile flaw, stitching irregularity, or transit defect, Aura Studio covers all reverse pickup and re-dispatch shipping costs. Please share clear photographs or video with our WhatsApp concierge within 48 hours of parcel receipt.

4. How to Initiate an Exchange

Step-by-Step Concierge Protocol:

  1. Message our dedicated concierge on WhatsApp at +92 321 4153059 with your Order Reference Number.
  2. Specify your requested replacement size, design, or alternative collection piece.
  3. Repack the item securely in its original packaging.
  4. Our designated courier partner will collect the return parcel from your doorstep.
  5. Upon atelier inspection, your replacement piece is dispatched with express delivery.
